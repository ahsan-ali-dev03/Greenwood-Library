About Greenwood

More than a building full of books — a home for curious minds.

Greenwood Public Library has been at the heart of the community since 1983. We believe everyone deserves access to knowledge, regardless of age, income, or background.

Beyond books, we host weekly events, reading challenges for children, book clubs, digital literacy workshops, and other community programs.

Our spaces are designed for quiet study, focused research, conversation, and discovery. Whether you are looking for your next great novel or a comfortable place to work, there is always room for you here.

Free Wi-Fi, public computers, and access to digital resources make the library a place where traditional collections meet modern technology.
